Despeckle drops stray clusters smaller than this many pixels. Thin details can't survive a big downscale — they shatter into orphans, and this sweeps them up before the outline wraps each one in a dark block.

Wall / floor

1

Surface mode draws at 416×112 — the exact wall and floor bands of the room. Outline, rim light and despeckle switch off, because a wall has no silhouette. Mirror reflects every other repeat, which is the cheapest way to stop a picture that doesn't tile from showing a seam.

Background

14

Flood-fills inward from the edges and clears anything close to the corner colour. Handles solid or near-solid backdrops — it won't lift a busy photo.

Edge

Rim light on the lit side, outline on the shadow side. Together they give every asset the same light direction.

Export

1× is the real sprite — that's what goes in the game, and what you paste into Paint when you want to keep working on it. 8× is the same pixels blown up with no blurring, for chat or anywhere it needs to be visible. Copy puts it on the clipboard; Download saves a file.

Send to room editor

Fits your sprite into the item's tile box and drops it straight into the room editor. Both pages must be open from the same address.
